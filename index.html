TECH TIPS

Make your laptop last longer

Simple habits that prevent the most common repairs we see.

Look after your battery

Avoid leaving your laptop at 100% on the charger all day, and keep it out of hot cars. If the battery swells, drains in minutes or stops charging, replace it. A swollen battery can damage the trackpad and casing.

Shop laptop batteries

Keep it cool

Use your laptop on a hard, flat surface, not a bed or cushion. A loud fan, hot keyboard or sudden shutdowns usually mean dust in the fan and old thermal paste. A clean-out restores performance and protects the motherboard.

Book a cleaning

Back up before it fails

Keep important files in two places: an external drive and a cloud service. Clicking sounds, slow file opening or disk errors are early warnings. Back up straight away and bring the drive to us.

Data recovery help

Upgrade instead of replacing

A slow laptop often only needs an SSD and more RAM, for far less than a new machine. Tell us your model and we will confirm which memory and drives it supports.

Questions before you buy

How do I find the right spare part?

Use the finder to choose your laptop brand, model and year, then the part type. Parts can differ within the same model, so check the part number printed on your old part or laptop label. Not sure? Send us a part request or a photo on WhatsApp and we will confirm it before you buy.

Do you deliver?

Yes, we deliver island-wide. Delivery is Rs. 450 and free on orders over Rs. 25,000. You can also collect free of charge from our Kaduwela store.

How can I pay?

Pay at the store when you collect, cash on delivery, or by bank transfer. We call you to confirm every order before it is dispatched.

Can you fit the part for me?

Yes. Order the part and mention fitting in your order notes, or bring your laptop to our store. We will fit and test it for you.

What if an item shows "Price on request"?

Prices for some items change with supplier stock. Tap "Ask for price" and we will reply with the current price and availability, usually the same day.

Can't find your part?

Send us your laptop model or a photo of the old part's label. We'll check our stock and suppliers and get back to you.
